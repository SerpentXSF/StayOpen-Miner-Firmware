## What changed

The only change from 2.0.22 is that the notice above now appears in the
miner's own web interface, at the top of the Firmware Update card, in English
and Chinese. It was already in the README and on the web flasher; it was not
in the one place an owner actually looks at.

No functional change. If you are on 2.0.22 there is no reason to update
except to have the notice in front of you.

## Still open

Stated here rather than left in a document nobody opens.

**The fan-fault protection cannot fire.** The flag that would trigger it is
never set, so a stalled or failed fan will not by itself shut the miner down.
The over-temperature trip and the unreadable-sensor trip are the two thermal
protections known to work. Do not treat a working fan as something the
firmware is checking for you.

**A BC04 cannot de-energise its own hashboard once the I²C bus is gone.**
The regulator is on that bus; with the bus dead the firmware has no way to
command the power off, and reboots do not help. A miner showing an empty bus
scan, a board temperature of −60 °C, or a reboot every four minutes should
have its power removed by hand, not left to cycle.

Both are described in `docs/HARDWARE-SAFETY.md`, along with what the firmware
does protect against.

**Updating through the web interface has been reported to fail.** One owner
could not apply updates from the web UI and had to use USB and the flasher.
Not reproduced here and not fixed, because it is not yet understood. If it
happens to you, the flasher is the way through, and a serial log of the
attempt would help more than anything else.

Two stratum defects found by reading the code are also still open and are
listed in `docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md`.

## Files

`-full.bin` is the whole flash and is what the web flasher writes over USB.
`-ota.bin` and `-www-ota.bin` are for updating in place. `SHA256SUMS` covers
every file here; check it before you flash something you downloaded.
